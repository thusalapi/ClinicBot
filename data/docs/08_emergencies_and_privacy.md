# Emergencies, Prescriptions and Patient Privacy

## Emergencies

Sunrise Family Clinic is not an emergency hospital. For chest pain, difficulty
breathing, severe bleeding, signs of stroke, or loss of consciousness, call 1990
(Suwa Seriya ambulance) or go to the nearest hospital emergency unit immediately.
The nearest emergency unit is at Colombo South Teaching Hospital, about 4 km away.

## Prescription refills

- Repeat prescriptions for long-term medicines (for example blood pressure or diabetes
  medicines) can be requested by WhatsApp. Allow one working day.
- A repeat prescription can be issued at most three times before a review consultation
  is required.
- Antibiotics, sleeping tablets and controlled drugs are never issued without a
  consultation.

## Medical certificates

Medical certificates (sick leave) are issued only after a consultation and cannot be
back-dated more than 2 days.

## Patient privacy

- Medical records are confidential and are shared only with the patient, or with a
  parent/guardian for patients under 16.
- Staff will never share test results or appointment details over the phone without
  verifying the patient's NIC number and date of birth.
- ClinicBot does not have access to medical records or test results.
- Patients can request a copy of their records at reception; copies are ready within
  5 working days.
