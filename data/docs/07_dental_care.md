## Before a dental extraction

- Eat a light meal 1–2 hours before the procedure.
- Tell the dentist if you take blood thinners (for example aspirin, warfarin or
  clopidogrel). Do not stop them without speaking to your doctor.
- Arrange for someone to accompany you if you are having a wisdom tooth removed.

## After an extraction

- Bite on the gauze for 30–45 minutes.
- Do not rinse, spit forcefully or use a straw for 24 hours.
- Avoid hot drinks and smoking for 48 hours.
- Contact the clinic if bleeding does not stop after 2 hours or if swelling gets worse
  after day 3.

## Children's dentistry

Children from age 3 can see the dentist. The first visit is a free "happy visit" to
get them comfortable with the chair.
