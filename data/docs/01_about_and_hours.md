# About Sunrise Family Clinic

Sunrise Family Clinic is a community clinic offering general practice, paediatrics,
women's health, dental care and an on-site laboratory.

## Address and contact

- Address: 42 Lake Road, Colombo 05
- Main phone: 011 234 5678
- WhatsApp (appointments only): 077 123 4567
- Email: hello@sunriseclinic.example

## Opening hours

| Day                | Hours           |
|--------------------|-----------------|
| Monday – Friday    | 7:30 AM – 9:00 PM |
| Saturday           | 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM |
| Sunday             | 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM (GP only) |
| Poya days          | Closed          |

The laboratory opens at 6:30 AM on weekdays for fasting blood tests.

## Parking

Free parking for 20 vehicles is available behind the building. Overflow parking is
available at the Lake Road public car park (Rs. 100 per hour), a two-minute walk away.

## Accessibility

The ground floor is fully wheelchair accessible. A lift serves the first floor
(dental and women's health). Wheelchairs can be borrowed at reception.
