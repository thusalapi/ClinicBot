# Vaccination Services

## Childhood vaccinations

We follow the national immunisation schedule. Vaccinations for children are given by
Dr. Ayesha Fernando during paediatric clinic hours. Please bring the Child Health
Development Record (CHDR) book to every visit.

Optional vaccines available for children (not part of the free national schedule):

- Chickenpox (varicella) — Rs. 9,500 per dose
- Hepatitis A — Rs. 6,800 per dose
- Influenza (yearly) — Rs. 4,200
- Pneumococcal (PCV) — Rs. 12,500 per dose

## Adult vaccinations

- Influenza — Rs. 4,200, available March to August
- Tetanus booster — Rs. 800
- Hepatitis B (3-dose course) — Rs. 3,000 per dose
- HPV — Rs. 18,000 per dose; recommended for ages 9–26

Adult vaccines are given by any GP; an appointment is recommended but not required.

## Travel vaccinations

Travel vaccines (typhoid, yellow fever certificate referral, rabies pre-exposure)
need a GP consultation at least 4 weeks before travel.

## After your vaccination

Please wait in the clinic for 15 minutes after any vaccine so staff can watch for
allergic reactions. Mild fever or soreness at the injection site for 1–2 days is
normal. Contact us if fever is above 39°C or lasts more than 2 days.
