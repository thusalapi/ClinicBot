# Appointments Policy

## How to book

Appointments can be booked by phone, by WhatsApp, at reception, or through the
ClinicBot assistant. Walk-in patients are seen for GP consultations only, and only
when there is a free slot. Specialist consultations always require an appointment.

## Arrival time

Please arrive 10 minutes before your appointment. First-time patients should arrive
20 minutes early to complete the registration form.

## Late arrival

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, your appointment may be moved to the next
available slot so that other patients are not delayed.

## Cancellation and rescheduling

- You can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before the appointment.
- Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the appointment incur a fee of Rs. 500.
- Patients who miss an appointment without notice (a "no-show") are charged Rs. 1,000.
- After three no-shows within 12 months, the patient must pay the consultation fee in
  advance when booking.

Fees are waived for documented medical emergencies.

## Bringing children

Patients under 16 years must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian.
