# Laboratory Services

The on-site laboratory is open Monday – Friday 6:30 AM – 7:00 PM and Saturday
7:00 AM – 2:00 PM. No appointment is needed for blood tests.

## Preparing for tests

### Fasting blood sugar (FBS) and lipid profile

- Do not eat or drink anything except plain water for 10–12 hours before the test.
- Take your usual morning medicines with water unless your doctor said otherwise.
- Diabetic patients on insulin should speak to their doctor before fasting.

### Urine full report (UFR)

- Collect a mid-stream sample of the first urine of the morning in the sterile
  container provided by the lab.
- Bring the sample to the lab within 2 hours.

### Thyroid function (TSH)

- No fasting needed. If you take thyroxine, have the blood drawn before your daily dose.

## Collecting results

Results are sent by SMS link when ready. Printed copies can be collected at the lab
counter with the receipt number.

## Home sample collection

Home collection is available within 10 km for Rs. 1,500 per visit. Book at least one
day ahead by phone.
