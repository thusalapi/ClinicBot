# Fees and Payments

## Consultation fees

| Service                              | Fee (Rs.) |
|--------------------------------------|-----------|
| GP consultation                      | 2,000     |
| Paediatric consultation              | 3,000     |
| Obstetrics & gynaecology consultation| 3,500     |
| Dental check-up                      | 2,500     |
| Dental cleaning (scaling)            | 4,500     |
| Follow-up visit within 14 days (same doctor, same issue) | 50% of the original fee |

A booking fee of Rs. 300 applies to appointments made by phone or WhatsApp. There is
no booking fee for appointments made at reception or through ClinicBot.

## Payment methods

We accept cash, Visa, Mastercard, Amex, and QR payments (LankaQR). Cheques are not
accepted.

## Insurance

We work directly with the following insurers for outpatient claims: Ceylinco,
AIA, Allianz and Union Assurance. Please bring your insurance card and NIC. For other
insurers, we provide an itemised receipt so you can claim yourself.

## Senior citizens

Patients aged 65 and over receive a 10% discount on consultation fees (not on
laboratory tests or medicines).
